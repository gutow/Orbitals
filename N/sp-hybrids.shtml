Visualizing sp hybrids and the p orbitals that are left
Below you may choose to display the hybrid orbitals sp+ and sp- plus the unhybridized orbitals 2py and 2pz that are left after combining the 2s and 2px orbitals to make the two sp hybrids. Use the menus to select up to four different orbitals to display at once, plus control their colors and display mode.
