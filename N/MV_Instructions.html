You can interact with the image. It is displayed using the JSmol.
Rotate the image Hold down the mouse button and drag the cursor over the image.
Zoom the view Hold down the shift key and the mouse button while dragging up or down over the image. On a touchpad two finger drag-up zooms in.
Many more options in the right-click menu The right-click menu can also be activated by clicking on the JSmol icon at the lower right of the viewer.
